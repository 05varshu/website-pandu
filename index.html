Northern Territory | Darwin

YSRCP Northern Territory (Darwin)is the official regional initiative connecting all YSR Fans diaspora in Northern Territory, Australia, with the core principles, welfare vision, and development goals of the YSR Congress Party (YSRCP).Rooted in the vision of Shri Y.S. Jagan Mohan Reddy, our chapter serves as a bridge for the Telugu community in Darwin and across the Northern Territory. We are dedicated to promoting social justice, community welfare, cultural unity, and active participation of Non-Resident Telugus in the development of Andhra Pradesh.
