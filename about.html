About Us

Rooted in the vision of Shri Y.S. Jagan Mohan Reddy, YSR Congress Party Northern Territory (Darwin) serves as a regional initiative connecting supporters and the Telugu community across the Northern Territory, Australia.

Our chapter acts as a bridge for the Telugu diaspora by promoting social justice, community welfare, cultural unity, and meaningful engagement among Non-Resident Telugus (NRTs). We strive to strengthen community bonds while encouraging active participation in initiatives that contribute to the development and progress of Andhra Pradesh.

Our Objectives

Community Unity

Bringing together Telugus residing across Darwin and the Northern Territory to foster strong cultural, social, and community relationships.

NRI Support & Welfare

Supporting local Telugu families, international students, and professionals through networking, guidance, and community assistance.

Promoting Welfare Governance

Sharing the vision, welfare initiatives, and public policies of the YSR Congress Party with the global Telugu community.

Cultural Preservation

Celebrating Telugu language, traditions, festivals, and cultural heritage while strengthening the identity of the Telugu community in Australia.

Our Vision

To build a united, empowered, and socially responsible Telugu community in Northern Territory by promoting service, cultural values, community engagement, and welfare-oriented initiatives inspired by the ideals of YSR Congress Party.

Our Mission

To connect Telugu people across the Northern Territory, encourage volunteerism, organize community and cultural programs, support new migrants and students, and strengthen the relationship between the Telugu diaspora and Andhra Pradesh through constructive engagement.
